I am a Computer Engineering student at Toronto Metropolitan University. Most of
what I work on sits close to the hardware: understanding how a program actually
executes on a processor, where its time goes, and what has to change to make it
faster.

## What interests me

I am drawn to the parts of computing where software meets the machine it runs
on. That includes systems programming in C and C++, software performance
analysis and optimization on Linux, computer architecture, and embedded systems
where the software and the board are designed together.

Practically, this has meant measuring a solver with Linux performance counters
to understand its cache behaviour, and designing power and telemetry hardware
for a rocketry flight computer. Both involve the same habit: form a hypothesis
about what the system is doing, measure it, and then change the implementation
based on what the measurement says.

## How I like to work

I prefer to build something, measure it, and explain the result. A project is
not finished for me when it runs; it is finished when I can describe why it
behaves the way it does and what I would change next.

I am currently looking for software, systems, and performance engineering
internship and co-op positions.
